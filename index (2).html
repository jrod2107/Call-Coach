      How the live coach is wired

      1. 1
        Sign in to the coach with your Supabase login (the same kind of login as CA Hub). Jake is the admin; Marie and Joe are CSRs.
      2. 2
        Connect RingCentral on the Live call tab. You sign in with your own RingCentral user; the coach registers as one of your phones. Done once per browser.
      3. 3
        Answer calls here. Incoming calls ring in the Phone card. Answering starts the call, the timer and live transcription at once.
      4. 4
        Listen. Your side and the customer's side go to Deepgram as two separate channels, so the coach knows who said what. Customer sentences feed the coach; your sentences tick off the checklist (fee quoted, access reminder, etc.).
      5. 5
        Coach. Same engine as before: next line to say, diagnostic and coached questions, fee, pushback answers. "Auto-coach" asks Claude every few customer sentences; off by default to save credit.
      6. 6
        Hang up. The transcript, facts and HCP note are saved to the calls table for review and coaching later.

      If something's off

      • Calls don't ring here: check the Phone card status. Reconnect RingCentral if the token expired (about every 7 days of no use). Your RingCentral user needs a digital line, and the app needs VoIP Calling permission.
      • They ring here and in the RingCentral app: that's normal. Answer in whichever you like; only calls answered here are transcribed.
      • Can't hear the customer: set playRemoteAudio: true in config.js.
      • No transcript appears: the browser needs microphone permission. Check the pill next to "On the call": it should say listening.
      • Ask Claude fails: the coach-suggest function or its ANTHROPIC_API_KEY secret isn't set up yet. Everything else still works.
